### The Challenge

A Kinangop-based milk packaging firm was struggling with rising feed costs and inefficient routing across its milk collection network. The business had been operating for over a decade but was entirely reliant on paper-based tracking, farmer-reported production values, and manual reconciliation — with no centralised data management in place.

The result was a growing gap between reported and actual production figures, difficulty identifying underperforming routes, and no visibility into cost trends over time.

### Our Solution

We conducted an in-depth financial and operational analysis covering 10 years of business records, examining inventory flow, revenue patterns, standout seasons, and statistical outliers. This gave us a clear picture of where value was being lost.

Our findings identified three core problem areas: inconsistent data capture at the farm level, gaps in inventory tracking between collection and packaging, and a revenue reconciliation process that was masking losses over time.

From there, we designed and implemented a structured intervention — combining staff training, a streamlined data collection system, and automated reporting tools tailored to the team's existing capacity.

### The Result

Within the first operating quarter post-implementation, the business recorded a **15% reduction in operational overhead** — primarily through tighter inventory control and the elimination of duplicate reporting errors.

Additional outcomes included improved route efficiency, reliable production forecasting for the first time, and a management dashboard giving the owners real-time visibility into daily operations.
