There is a pattern that repeats itself across Kenyan businesses of every size and sector. A company is growing —> customers are coming in, revenue is moving —> but no one can say with confidence how long this will last, which customers are most likely to return, or where the business will be in six months.

Decisions get made. But they get made on instinct, on experience, and sometimes on hope. After mixed success, as losses and stress start piling up, it stops being a business and becomes another job.

It’s the reality of running a business in an environment where time is short, margins are tight, and there simply isn’t a way for you to be in multiple places at once.

*Blueprint BI exists to change that.*

## What We Do

We are a business intelligence consultancy built specifically for Kenyan SMEs.

That specificity matters.

We do not offer a generic data service. We start from the ground up: asking the actual questions business owners need answered.

## Why Now

The tools have caught up. What used to require a large data team and significant infrastructure can now be done leaner, faster, and at a fraction of the cost.

A business with one salesperson and an assistant can have the same quality of insight that a business with two hundred employees had five years ago.

The gap is no longer technical. It is awareness and access.

Modern businesses are competing harder than ever—through social media and WhatsApp groups. However, this often leads to a lack of visibility for the owner. How can you tell if the business is truly profitable?

*That is the gap Blueprint BI is here to close.*

## What to Expect

Practical thinking grounded in the realities of doing business in Kenya.

We will write about common data mistakes and how to avoid them. About tricks and tools worth knowing. About what good decision-making looks like when you have limited time or budget. About the questions your data can answer that you have not thought to ask yet.

If any of that is useful to you, we are glad you are here.
